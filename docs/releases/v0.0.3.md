The brand moves from the retired orange (`#FF7A2E`, `v0.0.1`–`v0.0.2`) to the
palette the product was always meant to carry, taken from the Sir Collins portal
and retuned for a phone screen:

- **Ink** `#05060F` — icon tile, app background, terminal background.
- **Steel** `#9EC1E6` — the one primary accent: actions, cursor, selection, the
  mark on ink.
- **Steel strong** `#D8E9FF`, **steel deep** `#6A87B8` — pressed and selected
  states, containers.
- **Aurora status** — teal `#43C6C9`, violet `#9A8CFF`, rose `#FF9FBC`, soft
  green `#9FD4A8`, sand `#E6BE8A` (caution only).

Every surface was retuned together: Compose palette and Material scheme, the
terminal's default and light schemes, the built-in editor's CodeMirror theme,
notification colour, launcher icon and monochrome layer, website and social
card. Contrast was measured against the ink background: steel is 10.8:1, the
primary text 17.9:1, and even the faint label colour is 5.4:1.

`v0.0.1` and `v0.0.2` keep their published, orange assets. They are immutable:
this release is the first build with the intended identity, not a recolour of an
old artifact.

### Packaging

The APK now filters native libraries to the two ABIs the product actually runs
on, `arm64-v8a` and `x86_64`. A few dependencies ship 32-bit copies of helper
libraries; without the filter a 32-bit device could install the app and then
fail to start PRoot. `android:extractNativeLibs` and the `targetSdk 28` pin are
unchanged (ADR-0002).

### Documentation

- `BRAND.md` now states the palette, the reason for it, and the asset inventory.
- New governance and delivery records: `docs/strategy/GOVERNANCE.md`,
  `THREAT-MODEL.md`, `REQUIREMENTS.md`, `DEVIATIONS.md`, `PERSONAS.md`;
  `docs/DISTRIBUTION.md`, `SECURITY.md`, `PRIVACY.md`, `ACCESSIBILITY.md`;
  `MAINTAINERS`; and ADR-0002 for the pinned `targetSdk 28`.
- `REQUIREMENTS.md` claims **P0 (partial)** and **P1 (partial)** only. It does
  not claim P2: the QVAC engine ships, but the Ask/Explain/Fix surface is not
  exposed and inference has not run on a physical arm64 device.

### Known limitation

The website screenshots were captured on `v0.0.2` and still show the orange
interface. They will be replaced once a physical device capture exists; the
build in this release is steel throughout.
